# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

commUNITY is intended to be an open, welcoming and collaborative project.

We are committed to creating an environment where people can participate regardless of their:

* Technical experience
* Professional background
* Community experience
* Geographic location
* Language
* Level of involvement in the project

We expect everyone participating in the project to treat others with respect.

> **Build the infrastructure. Empower the community. Let the community govern it.**

That principle applies to how we build the project as well as what we build.

---

# Expected Behavior

Contributors and community members are expected to:

* Be respectful and constructive.
* Assume good intentions while remaining open to evidence.
* Listen to different perspectives.
* Ask questions before making assumptions.
* Provide constructive feedback.
* Accept reasonable criticism.
* Explain disagreements respectfully.
* Focus discussions on ideas and outcomes.
* Give appropriate credit to other contributors.
* Respect community decisions.
* Help create an environment where new contributors feel comfortable participating.

---

# Healthy Disagreement

Disagreement is an important part of an open-source project.

We encourage people to challenge:

* Requirements
* Architecture
* Product decisions
* Governance models
* Technical assumptions
* Security approaches
* Contribution models

A disagreement about an idea is not a disagreement with the person.

Good:

> "I don't think this governance model will work because..."

Not:

> "Anyone who thinks this is a good idea doesn't understand governance."

Strong technical disagreement is welcome.

Personal attacks are not.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment
* Personal attacks
* Threats
* Intimidation
* Discrimination
* Hate speech
* Deliberate humiliation
* Sexual harassment
* Publishing someone's private information without permission
* Sharing confidential community information
* Deliberately disrupting discussions
* Trolling or sustained bad-faith participation
* Retaliation against someone who raises a concern
* Impersonating another contributor or community member

---

# Privacy and Personal Information

commUNITY may involve discussions about residents, workers, communities and community operations.

Respect personal privacy at all times.

Do not publicly share:

* Personal identification information
* Aadhaar information
* Phone numbers
* Home addresses
* Private financial information
* Private communications
* Background-verification information
* Authentication credentials
* Private community records

unless the person has explicitly authorized the disclosure and it is appropriate for the context.

---

# Community Discussions

When participating in GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, UIP reviews or other project forums:

* Stay relevant to the topic.
* Avoid repetitive arguments.
* Do not deliberately derail discussions.
* Take extended disagreements to a more appropriate forum when necessary.
* Keep criticism focused on the proposal or implementation.
* Avoid making assumptions about another contributor's intentions.

Maintainers may close discussions when they become unproductive.

---

# Governance Discussions

commUNITY uses the **Unity Improvement Proposal (UIP)** process for significant decisions.

Participants are encouraged to:

* Review proposals carefully.
* Ask questions.
* Identify risks.
* Provide evidence.
* Suggest alternatives.
* Respect the final governance process.

Voting outcomes do not prevent future proposals from being submitted.

A rejected proposal may be improved and proposed again when new information or a substantially different approach exists.

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for maintaining a healthy project environment.

Depending on the situation, maintainers may:

1. Ask participants to modify their behavior.
2. Remove inappropriate comments or content.
3. Close discussions or issues.
4. Temporarily restrict participation.
5. Remove participation privileges.
6. Take other reasonable action necessary to protect the community.

Actions should be proportionate to the behavior and circumstances.

---

# Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately to the project maintainers through the security/contact mechanism provided by the repository.

Please do not publicly expose personal information about the people involved.

Reports should contain enough information for maintainers to understand what happened, including relevant links or context where appropriate.

Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is not acceptable.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project-related spaces and activities, including:

* GitHub repositories
* Issues
* Pull Requests
* Discussions
* UIP reviews
* Community meetings
* Project events
* Other spaces explicitly associated with commUNITY

---

# Final Principle

commUNITY is intended to demonstrate the kind of community it aims to help create.

We want a community where people can:

**Question freely.**

**Disagree respectfully.**

**Contribute openly.**

**Learn from one another.**

**Build together.**

> **People create communities.
> commUNITY provides the infrastructure that helps them thrive.**
