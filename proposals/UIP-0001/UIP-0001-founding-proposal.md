# UIP-0001: commUNITY — Founding Proposal

**Status:** Draft
**Type:** Foundational
**Author:** commUNITY Community
**Created:** 2026-08-15
**Version:** 0.1

---

## 1. Abstract

commUNITY is an open-source platform designed to help residential communities build trusted, transparent, participative and self-governed communities.

The platform aims to bring together community services, verified community identity, initiatives and campaigns, classifieds, recommendations, knowledge sharing, community contributions, governance and transparent financial representation into a single community-owned platform.

commUNITY is designed around a simple principle:

> **Technology should help communities trust, participate, contribute and govern themselves.**

This proposal establishes the initial vision and principles of commUNITY and seeks community approval to proceed with defining and building the platform through subsequent Unity Improvement Proposals (UIP).

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Residential communities already perform many activities collectively, but these activities are often fragmented across:

* WhatsApp groups
* Spreadsheets
* Paper records
* Individual administrators
* Informal recommendations
* Personal contacts
* Bank statements
* Physical notice boards

This creates several challenges:

### Trust

There is often no reliable way to establish that a person participating in the community is actually a resident, authorized staff member or verified service provider.

### Transparency

Community initiatives and financial activities may depend heavily on manual records and individual communication.

### Participation

Residents who voluntarily contribute significant time, knowledge or effort to the community may have no persistent record of that contribution.

### Knowledge

Useful community knowledge—such as government procedures, trusted services, schools, doctors and vendors—is frequently lost when residents move away or information becomes buried in chat groups.

### Governance

Community decisions are often dependent on informal processes, making it difficult to establish a transparent history of proposals, participation and decisions.

---

## 3. Vision

commUNITY aims to provide an open-source digital infrastructure for residential communities where:

* Community members are verified.
* Community services are trusted.
* Community initiatives are transparent.
* Community knowledge is preserved.
* Contributions are recognized.
* Financial records can be independently verified.
* Governance is participative and transparent.
* Communities retain control over their own data and operations.

---

## 4. Guiding Principles

### 4.1 Community First

The platform exists to serve the community, not the other way around.

### 4.2 Verified Participation

A community should know who is participating in it.

Self-registration alone should not be sufficient to obtain community membership.

### 4.3 Transparency by Design

Important community activities should have an auditable history wherever appropriate.

### 4.4 Contribution Matters

Voluntary contributions of time, knowledge, skills and leadership should be recognized.

### 4.5 One Person Should Not Buy Governance Power

Financial contribution should not automatically translate into greater governance influence.

### 4.6 Privacy by Default

Personal and sensitive information should remain off-chain and should only be accessible to authorized parties.

### 4.7 Blockchain Where It Adds Trust

Blockchain should be used selectively where immutability and independent verification provide meaningful value.

It should not be used merely because a feature can technically be implemented on-chain.

### 4.8 Open Source

The platform should remain open to community participation, review, contribution and extension.

### 4.9 Community-Owned Data

The software and the data generated by a community are separate concerns.

The community should retain control over its own data.

### 4.10 Incremental Governance

The governance model should evolve through transparent proposals rather than being defined completely upfront.

## 5. Proposed Capabilities

commUNITY is initially envisioned to provide the following capabilities.

### 5.1 Community Identity

A trusted identity and membership layer for:

* Owners
* Residents
* Tenants
* Society administrators
* Committee members
* Community volunteers
* Verified workers
* Vendors and service providers

Community members should be onboarded through an internal verification and approval process.

---

### 5.2 Verified Community Services

Communities should be able to maintain a trusted directory of workers and service providers such as:

* Watchmen
* Maids
* Drivers
* Plumbers
* Electricians
* Other community service providers

Where legally and operationally appropriate, verification information can be recorded as a verification status rather than exposing sensitive personal information.

---

### 5.3 Community Campaigns

Residents and committees should be able to create and manage community initiatives such as:

* Ganesh Utsavam
* Road development
* Water pipeline projects
* Community events
* Infrastructure improvements
* Social initiatives

Campaigns may contain:

* Objective
* Budget
* Tasks
* Volunteers
* Contributions
* Expenses
* Progress
* Completion status

---

### 5.4 Community Classifieds

Residents should be able to share opportunities and items within the verified community, including:

* Job opportunities
* Services
* Items for sale
* Items wanted
* Rentals
* Lost and found
* Other community announcements

---

### 5.5 Car Pooling

Residents should be able to create and discover:

* One-time rides
* Recurring rides
* Ride-sharing requests
* Available seats
* Common routes

---

### 5.6 Community Recommendations

Residents should be able to share recommendations for:

* Doctors
* Schools
* Hospitals
* Tutors
* Vendors
* Service providers
* Other community resources

Recommendations should prioritize transparency and community trust rather than paid rankings.

---

### 5.7 Community Knowledge Base

commUNITY should provide a persistent repository for useful community knowledge.

Examples include:

* Aadhaar procedures
* Voter ID procedures
* Passport processes
* Government office procedures
* Property-related procedures
* Local authority processes
* Frequently used government forms
* Community-specific operating procedures

Knowledge should ideally include:

* Steps
* Required documents
* Relevant offices/websites
* Last verified date
* Contributor
* Community verification status

## 6. Community Contribution Credits [FUTURE]

commUNITY proposes the concept of **Community Contribution Credits (CCC)**.

CCC would represent verified voluntary contributions made by community members.

Potential contribution categories include:

### Participation

* Community meetings
* Community discussions
* Events
* Surveys

### Service

* Volunteer work
* Helping other residents
* Community maintenance activities
* Government coordination

### Expertise

* Technical guidance
* Educational guidance
* Legal or procedural knowledge
* Professional expertise

### Leadership

* Campaign leadership
* Project ownership
* Community initiatives
* Conflict resolution
* Coordination with external organizations

The detailed contribution rubric, scoring methodology and verification mechanism will be defined through a future UIP.

CCC should initially be:

* Non-transferable
* Non-financial
* Non-purchasable
* Non-saleable

CCC should represent **community contribution**, not wealth.

---

## 7. Contribution and Future Governance

One of the long-term goals of commUNITY is to provide communities with a transparent history of voluntary contribution.

A resident who consistently contributes time, expertise and leadership should have a verifiable record of that contribution.

This information may eventually become part of a candidate's community profile when the individual chooses to participate in community elections or leadership positions.

However:

> **A contribution score should not automatically determine a person's eligibility, character or election outcome.**

The exact relationship between contribution history and governance will be defined through future UIPs and community approval.

---

## 8. Community Treasury

commUNITY proposes a transparent digital representation of the community treasury.

Actual community funds would continue to exist within existing financial systems such as:

* Bank accounts
* UPI
* Cash
* Cheques
* Other legally supported payment mechanisms

commUNITY would maintain a digital ledger representing:

* Income
* Expenses
* Opening balance
* Closing balance
* Campaign expenses
* Ledger history

---

## 9. Blockchain Representation

commUNITY proposes using blockchain as a verification and transparency
layer for approved community treasury ledger states.

Blockchain should not contain:

- Aadhaar information
- Personal financial information
- Resident private information
- Sensitive worker information
- Private community discussions

Instead, the blockchain may contain a verifiable representation of
approved ledger states.

Conceptually:

Actual Financial Activity
          |
          v
Bank / UPI / Cash / Cheque
          |
          v
Community Ledger
          |
          v
Treasurer Approval
          |
          v
Ledger State / Hash
          |
          v
Blockchain

## 10. Governance

commUNITY itself should evolve through an open proposal process.

Significant changes should be proposed through a **Unity Improvement Proposal (UIP)**.

Examples:

```text
UIP-0001  Founding Proposal
UIP-0002  Community Identity & Onboarding
UIP-0003  Contribution Credit Framework
UIP-0004  Treasury & Blockchain Architecture
UIP-0005  Governance Framework
```

A proposed lifecycle is:

```text
Draft
  ↓
Discussion
  ↓
Proposed
  ↓
Voting
  ↓
Accepted / Rejected
  ↓
Implementation
```

The exact voting mechanism, eligibility, quorum and approval thresholds will be established through a future governance UIP.

---

## 11. Open Source

commUNITY is intended to be developed as an open-source project.

The initial software license proposed for the project is:

**Apache License 2.0**

The software license, community governance and community-owned data are separate concerns.

The open-source license grants rights to use and modify the software. It does not grant ownership or unrestricted access to personal information or community data.

---

## 12. What This Proposal Does Not Decide

UIP-0001 intentionally does not finalize:

* Detailed technical architecture
* Database design
* API specifications
* Smart-contract implementation
* Contribution-credit formula
* Voting algorithm
* Election mechanism
* Identity-verification providers
* Background-verification providers
* Mobile application architecture
* Hosting model
* Multi-community federation
* Commercial/business model

These should be addressed through future UIPs.

## 13. Initial Scope

The initial version of commUNITY is proposed to focus on:

1. Verified community identity
2. Community services
3. Community campaigns
4. Classifieds
5. Car pooling
6. Community recommendations
7. Community knowledge
8. Contribution tracking
9. Community treasury ledger
10. Blockchain-based ledger representation
11. Proposal-based governance

---

## 14. Out of Scope for the Initial Version

The following are intentionally out of scope for the first version:

* CC Camera (Bird Eye views)
* Automated election outcomes
* Automated background verification
* AI-based governance decisions

---

## 15. Proposal

This UIP proposes that the community:

1. Recognize commUNITY as an open-source initiative for trusted and self-governed residential communities.
2. Approve the vision and guiding principles described in this proposal.
3. Approve verified community onboarding as a foundational principle.
4. Approve community services, campaigns, knowledge sharing and classifieds as initial platform capabilities.
5. Approve the concept of Community Contribution Credits.
6. Approve transparent community treasury representation as a platform capability.
7. Approve Polygon as the proposed initial blockchain representation layer for the treasury, subject to detailed technical review in a future UIP.
8. Adopt UIPs as the mechanism for evolving the platform's major decisions.
9. Authorize the creation of subsequent UIPs for detailed governance, identity, contribution, treasury and technical architecture.

**UIP-0003 — Community Contribution Credit Framework**

---

## 19. Closing Statement

commUNITY is not intended to be simply another apartment-management application.

The long-term vision is to create an open-source foundation through which communities can build:

**Trust → Participation → Contribution → Transparency → Governance**

The first step is not to build everything.

The first step is to agree on the principles by which the community should build it.

**This proposal requests that the community take that first step.**
